Road to December 6, 202622-Week Pass Plan — built for your situation

The one number that decides everything: You need 95 / 180 overall — AND ≥ 19 / 60 in each section separately. A brilliant total can't save a Reading score of 18. This entire plan is weighted toward protecting your Reading minimum.

Your three sections

SectionWhat it coversYour risk
Language KnowledgeVocab, kanji, grammarMedium
Reading 読解Info-retrieval, longer passages, connective flowHIGH — your real failure risk
Listening 聴解Conversations, monologuesLower (you live in Japan)

Targets to build toward

~1,800–2,000new N3 vocabulary words — ~100/week, ~15/day
~300new kanji (cumulative ~650) — ~15/week
~150N3 grammar points — ~8–10/week
Reading staminafrom "I can decode" → "extract answer under time pressure"

Core resources — pick and commit

Primary spine: Shin Kanzen Master N3 series (gold standard). Prioritize the Reading 読解 book first. Add Grammar and Vocab. Alternative if too steep: Nihongo Sou Matome N3 (gentler) → graduate to Shin Kanzen by Month 3.
Daily SRS (non-negotiable, on your commute): Anki or jpdb.io for vocab/kanji (N3 deck) + Bunpro for grammar SRS.
Reading practice (your priority): Satori Reader daily — best for connective flow, targets your direct weakness. NHK News Web Easy / Todai app for free info-retrieval practice.
Listening: Shin Kanzen / Sou Matome N3 listening sets + podcasts: Nihongo con Teppei (Intermediate), YUYU Nihongo. Eavesdrop deliberately in Noda/Tokyo — read every sign.
Mock exams: Official JLPT workbook 公式問題集 — save the full one for late October. Past-paper compilations for timed practice from Phase 4.

Weekly rhythm — 8–10 focused hours/week

Shape matters more than the total
Weekday morningsCommute (~30–40 min): SRS reps — Anki/jpdb vocab + Bunpro grammar. Pure maintenance, no thinking required.
Weekday evenings2–3 nights, ~45 min each: One focused block — grammar lesson + drill, OR a reading passage worked with full breakdown.
Saturday~2–2.5 hrs: Heavy session — new grammar batch + timed reading set + listening practice.
Sunday~1.5–2 hrs: Review week's mistakes + one Satori Reader long piece + light listening. Lower intensity.
Rule: Reading appears every single day, even if only one passage. It's a stamina skill — it decays without daily contact. This is the single most important habit in the plan.

The 5 phases

Phase 1 Foundation Repair & RampWeeks 1–4 · ~Jul 6 – Aug 2

Theme: close the N4 gaps the July exam exposed, start the N3 vocab engine.

  • Take your July results and list every weak sub-skill. Fix N4 holes first — N3 reading collapses if N4 grammar is shaky.
  • Start the vocab SRS engine NOW and never stop it. 15/day.
  • Begin Bunpro N3 grammar from the top.
  • Reading: start Satori Reader at an easy setting. Goal here is habit, not difficulty.
Checkpoint (end of Wk 4): SRS running daily without fail · ~60 N3 grammar points seen · comfortable with one Satori article a day.
Phase 2 Grammar Core & Reading MechanicsWeeks 5–9 · ~Aug 3 – Sep 6

Theme: build the grammar backbone and learn HOW to read for the test.

  • Push grammar hard — bulk of new grammar acquisition. Aim ~10 points/week.
  • Reading technique: start Shin Kanzen Master Reading N3. Learn the mechanics: skim for the question first, locate info, track が／は／のに／ため／そこで connective logic. Your weakness is connective flow — drill it explicitly.
  • Word-usage-in-context drills: don't just memorize words, study them in example sentences.
  • ⚠️ Register for the exam when JEES opens (late Aug). Do not skip this.
Checkpoint (end of Wk 9): ~half of N3 grammar internalized · can work a short reading passage with technique, not just brute force.
Phase 3 Reading Depth & Listening BuildWeeks 10–14 · ~Sep 7 – Oct 11

Theme: longer passages, real speed, ramp listening.

  • Move to longer reading passages — the 中文・長文 sections. This is your make-or-break zone.
  • Introduce timing: a passage you used to do in 8 min, now do in 5.
  • Ramp listening seriously — daily audio, shadowing, Shin Kanzen listening sets.
  • Finish the bulk of new grammar; shift grammar to review mode.
Checkpoint (end of Wk 14): Reading a 長文 passage and answering correctly under loose time pressure · listening no longer feels like a wall.
Phase 4 Integration & Timed SectionsWeeks 15–18 · ~Oct 12 – Nov 8

Theme: stop studying skills, start practicing the exam.

  • Do full timed sections — a complete Reading section in its real time limit, complete Listening, etc.
  • First full mock exam around Week 16. Score each section. Find which one is closest to the 19 floor — that's where the last weeks go.
  • Aggressively review every wrong answer. Mistakes are the curriculum now.
Checkpoint (end of Wk 18): Mock total comfortably over 95, AND every section clearing 19+ with margin.
Phase 5 Mock Blitz & TaperWeeks 19–22 · ~Nov 9 – Dec 5

Theme: simulate, repair, peak — then rest.

  • Full mock exams under exam conditions (use official 公式問題集). Aim for 2–3 complete runs.
  • Each mock → identify weakest section → spend the next days there.
  • Keep SRS reps going but stop adding large new batches by late November — consolidate what you have.
  • Final 3 days: light review only. Confirm exam venue, ID, time. No cramming. Sleep.
Dec 6 — Exam day. Trust the 22 weeks.

Weak-area drills — do these throughout

Information-retrieval reading: Read the question first, then scan the passage for the specific answer. Speed-scan exercises with NHK Easy / Todai — find a named fact in under 30 seconds.
Word usage in context: Never learn a word as a flashcard alone. Always pair with a sentence. The N3 vocab section tests which word fits the context, not raw recognition.
Longer reading / connective flow: Satori Reader daily + a weekly Shin Kanzen 長文 passage where you map connectors (しかし／そのため／一方／つまり) and summarize each paragraph in one line.
Honest expectation: A narrow N4 pass → comfortable N3 in ~22 weeks of disciplined 8–10 hr/week work is a realistic, achievable target. Living in Japan accelerates listening and vocab-in-context. Reading is where the environment helps least — which is exactly why this plan front-loads it every single day. Follow the rhythm, protect the Reading minimum, register on time. December is very winnable.
